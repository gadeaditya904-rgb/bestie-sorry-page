I'm Sorry, Bestie

This is just a little message for you, my best friend… 🥺❤️

Hey Bestie ❤️

I wanted to make this page because a simple "Sorry" doesn't feel enough for everything I want to tell you.

I know I made a mistake, and I'm genuinely sorry for the times when my words, my behaviour, or something I said has hurt you.

Sometimes I speak without thinking properly. Sometimes I use the wrong words, and only later realize that those words may have hurt you.

And honestly, I feel really bad about that.

I want you to know that hurting you was never what I wanted.

You Know Why I Am So Comfortable With You? ❤️

With most people, I think before I speak. I control some things about myself. I think about how I should behave, what I should say, and what I shouldn't say.

But with you…

I can just be myself.

I don't feel like I have to pretend to be someone else. I can talk about my life, share random things, laugh about stupid things, and simply behave like the real me.

And maybe because I'm so comfortable with you, sometimes I become careless with my words.

I say something without thinking enough about how it might sound or how it might make you feel.

But please don't ever think that my comfort means I don't care.

It's actually because I trust you so much and feel so comfortable being my real self around you.

But I also understand something important.

Being comfortable with you is never an excuse for me to hurt you.

So if I said something wrong, used words I shouldn't have used, or behaved in a way that hurt you, I'm truly sorry.

You didn't deserve those words. You didn't deserve to feel bad because of something I said.

I can't take those moments back, but I can learn from them.

From now on, I'll try to think more before I speak and be more careful with my words.

I don't want to make the same mistake again.

Our Friendship Is Really Special ❤️

Our friendship is honestly one of the most special things in my life.

You're the one person I can share so many things with. Things I don't always feel comfortable sharing with everyone else.

I can tell you about my life, my thoughts, random things, serious things, stupid things… basically anything. 😅❤️

And I love that we have that kind of comfort and trust between us.

I know we both want this friendship to stay with us forever.

And honestly…
I want that too. ❤️

I don't want one mistake, one argument, or a few wrong words from me to become bigger than everything beautiful we've built together.

Ek Goshta Sangaychi Hoti… 🥺❤️

Mala tula manvayla khup avadta yaar… 😅❤️

Majhyakadun chuka hotat, kahi vela mi chukicha bolto, kahi words wrong use karto… pan tula hurt karaycha maza kadhich intention nasto.

Actually…

Tujhya sobat mi itka comfortable asto ki mi majha real self rahu shakto.

Ani mhanunach kadhi kadhi majhyakadun chuka hotat.

Pan ek goshta nakkich…

Same mistake parat karaychi nahi.

Tu majhyasathi khup special ahes yaar. ❤️

"With you, I don't have to pretend to be someone else.

I can simply be myself." ❤️

I'm not writing all of this to make you feel bad.

I'm not expecting you to forget everything immediately.

I'm just trying to tell you honestly how much I value you and our friendship.

If I could go back and change the words that hurt you, I would.

Since I can't change the past, I'll try to change what I do from here.

I'll think more before I speak. I'll be more careful with my words. And most importantly…

I don't want to hurt you like that again.

❤️ Thank you for being you.

Thank you for being the person with whom I can be myself.

Thank you for listening to me, understanding me, and being there for me.

I genuinely don't want to lose this friendship.

I want us to keep making memories, keep laughing, keep talking about everything, and keep this friendship for a very, very long time. ❤️

No matter how imperfect I am sometimes, please know that my friendship with you is something I truly value.

I'm really sorry, Bestie.

From the bottom of my heart. 🥺❤️🫂

Made with a truly sorry heart ❤️
